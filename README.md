I talk AI systems into doing things they shouldn't. With permission, and an invoice.

I'm Luca Sambucci, independent AI security consultant in Rome and founder of [Noctive Security](https://www.noctivesecurity.com). The short version of the job: LLM and agent red-teaming, prompt injection, jailbreaks, attack automation.

My first computer arrived in 1984, when I was ten. By the end of the decade I was taking apart computer viruses and writing about them in magazines, and I never left security after that. In 2017 I started studying AI to understand what it would do to my field; the two threads have since merged into a single job: finding out what an AI system can be talked into, before someone less polite does.

Today that means red-team engagements and advisory work, for companies and for institutions. I've consulted for the European Commission's Joint Research Centre and the European Defence Agency, advised Italian government bodies, and served (pro bono) in the EU AI Office working groups that drafted the first Code of Practice for general-purpose AI. I also teach, and speak at the occasional event.
